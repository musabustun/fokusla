Thanks for reviewing Fokus, a study tracker for Turkish students preparing for the national university entrance exam (YKS). The UI is Turkish.

Resubmission: the screenshots/search-result images and promotional text that mentioned price were replaced, and the Support URL is now a real support page with an email contact.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED. On first launch: tap "Başla" (Get started) -> choose any exam field (e.g. SAY), "Devam" -> keep the default theme, "Devam" -> "Şimdilik atla" (Skip for now). The app is then fully usable; only Social needs sign-in.

Main features:
- Focus tab: tap start (a full-screen, StandBy-style view opens; swipe sideways for pages; tap for controls, "küçült" minimizes; long-press to customize). On a device, a Live Activity shows while a session runs. Focus sounds are generated on the device.
- Apple Watch: start, break ("Mola"), resume ("Devam") and finish (tap for buttons); swipe between three faces; syncs with the iPhone via WatchConnectivity. Background mode "mindfulness" (WKExtendedRuntimeSession) runs only during a focus session, to keep the timer on screen (dimmed in Always On). No HealthKit/workout session.
- iMessage app: "Bugün" shares a card with today's study time; "Oda" prepares a study-room invitation. Nothing is sent until the user taps Send.
- Analysis: add a test result; net = correct - wrong/4. Books: add a book (optional barcode scan uses the camera on device; no image is stored or sent). Tasks: add a task. Settings: goal, local reminders, themes, export (JSON), "delete all data".

SOCIAL (optional; on iPhone "Bugün" tab > "Diğer" > "Sosyal"; on iPad in the sidebar): needs Sign in with Apple AND an iCloud account on the device. The user picks a username and adds friends with a one-time invite link/QR or by exact username. After acceptance, friends see each other's username, focus/break status and today's study time; "rooms" let people study in parallel. There is no search, discovery, feed, chat, photos or free text except username and room name (filtered for offensive words).
- Safety (1.2): "..." menu on each request/friend: "Engelle" (block) and "Bildir" (report; opens our support page with email). "Sosyalde görün" hides the user.
- Account deletion (5.1.1(v)): Social profile > "Sosyal profilimi sil"; also Settings > "Hesabı sil".
- With one device you can create a profile, an invite link and a room; seeing a friend needs a second iCloud account.
- Data: Apple CloudKit only, no server of ours. Friend/room data is in users' private databases (CKShare). The public database holds only a hash of the username, an encryption key, a random mailbox id and end-to-end encrypted requests; hence the privacy label "User ID, App Functionality, not tracking".

Spotify is OPTIONAL: Spotify's iOS App Remote only controls playback in the user's Spotify app. The first connect switches to Spotify for consent (OAuth PKCE, no client secret); tokens stay in the device Keychain. Our Spotify app is in Development mode, so the reviewer's account may not connect; Fokus then shows a plain "Spotify'da aç" link.

Privacy: no analytics, tracking or ads. Study data lives on the device; with sync on it goes only to the user's own private CloudKit database. No in-app purchases. Encryption: Apple system encryption/CryptoKit and HTTPS only; ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO. Open source (MIT): https://github.com/musabustun/fokusla
